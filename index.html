Welcome to PIEAS

Nestled in a serene countryside, built for the future

PIEAS offers BS, MS, MPhil and PhD programs — with postgraduate programs leading the way — plus specialized short courses for engineers, scientists and professionals. Highly qualified faculty and excellent infrastructure serve national and regional needs through education, research and development.
